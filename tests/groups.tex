% Groups and boxes in the text of a block, and a caption in the image.
%  G1. a paragraph that starts inside a group ({\bfseries ...} text):
%      its shape is set again when the group ends, image on either side;
%  G2. a tcolorbox between two paragraphs, beside the image: it takes the
%      width left there, breakable or not, image on either side;
%  G3. a caption (\captionof) in an image with a width: the image is then
%      a minipage of that width;
%  G4. two breakable tcolorboxes with no blank line between them, the first
%      beside the image, the second below it: the paragraph of the first
%      ends in the group of the second, and its narrowed shape was left
%      behind for the second (0.1.5: shifted and too wide, overfull box),
%      image on either side;
%  G5. a centred tcolorbox narrower than the line (width=.8\linewidth,
%      center), right after a paragraph with no blank line: it came while
%      the paragraph was still open, and kept the full line (0.1.5: it ran
%      over the image), image on either side, then with a deferred image;
%  G6. a block after a deep box (a tabular centred on its axis), its image
%      taller than its text, then a heading: TeX puts \lineskip after the
%      deep box, the first line of the block came lower than computed, and
%      the heading ran into the image (0.1.5);
%  G7. a block that opens with a tcolorbox, after a blank line: the box
%      starts its own paragraph after \nointerlineskip, and the image hung
%      on it went up into the line before (breakable) or down below the
%      box (not breakable) (0.1.5), image on either side.
% run.zsh checks that there is no error, no overfull box, and that no word
% overlaps an image.
